## Core Idea

Benchmarks give context to performance data. Without them, a 1.5% CTR could seem good or bad depending on the platform and industry. These are reference points — not targets.

---

## Meta Ads Benchmarks (2024–2025 averages)

| Metric | Average | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (all industries) | 0.9% | 1.5% | 2.5%+ |
| CPC | £0.80–£1.50 | £0.50–£0.80 | <£0.50 |
| CVR (to lead) | 8–10% | 12–15% | 20%+ |
| ROAS (ecomm) | 2–3x | 4–5x | 6x+ |

## Email Benchmarks

| Metric | Average | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Open Rate | 20–25% | 35%+ |
| CTR | 2–3% | 5%+ |
| Conversion Rate | 1–2% | 3%+ |

---

## How to Use These

- Use as **diagnostic tools**, not goals
- If CTR is below average, prioritise [[Hook Writing]] tests
- If CVR is below average, look at body copy and [[CTA Writing]]
- Feed benchmark gaps into [[AI Suggestions Engine]] to prioritise improvements

---

*Note: Benchmarks vary significantly by industry, audience temperature, and creative quality. Update this note as new data emerges.*
