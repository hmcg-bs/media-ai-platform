## Core Idea

Ad copy that reflects current cultural moments, platform behaviours, and creative trends performs better. This note tracks signals worth watching and incorporating into copy suggestions.

---

## Creative & Copy Trends (2025)

### Platform Behaviour Shifts
- **TikTok-native language** bleeding into Meta ads — casual, first-person, imperfect
- **Authenticity over polish** — lo-fi UGC outperforming studio-produced creative
- **Comment-reading hooks** — "POV: you just found out..." and "Tell me you [X] without telling me you [X]"
- **Duet / reaction format** repurposed for brand ads

### Copy Style Trends
- **Anti-ad tone** — Copy that acknowledges being an ad performs better with ad-fatigued audiences
- **Micro-specificity** — Hyper-specific numbers and details outperform general claims
- **Founder-voice** — Brands writing in the voice of their founder rather than "the brand"
- **Before/After framing** still strong, especially in health, finance, productivity niches

### Platform-Specific Signals
- **LinkedIn:** Contrarian takes and strong opinions outperform tips-and-tricks
- **Meta:** Video hooks under 3 seconds with on-screen text are winning
- **Google:** Conversational search queries rising — copy should match natural language
- **Email:** Plain-text emails outperforming designed templates in B2B
