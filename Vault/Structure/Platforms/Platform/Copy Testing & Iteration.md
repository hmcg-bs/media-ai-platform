# Copy Testing & Iteration

**Type:** Platform Note  
**Links:** [[Platform Overview]] · [[Performance Ads]] · [[Hook Writing]] · [[AI Suggestions Engine]] · [[Industry Benchmarks]]

---

## Core Idea

Good copy is found, not written. The first version is a hypothesis. Testing reveals what actually works, and iteration compounds the gains over time.

---

## The Testing Loop

```
Write variants → Launch with small budget → Measure → Kill losers → Scale winners → Write new variants
```

---

## What to Test (Priority Order)

1. **Hook / Headline** — Highest impact variable; test this first
2. **Offer framing** — "Save £500/month" vs "Earn back 10 hours a week"
3. **CTA** — "Start free" vs "Try it now" vs "Get your free audit"
4. **Body copy length** — Short punchy vs longer story-led
5. **Tone** — Direct/urgent vs calm/authoritative vs playful

---

## A/B Test Rules

- Test **one variable at a time** — multiple changes make results unreadable
- Run tests to **statistical significance** (minimum 500 impressions per variant)
- Use **equal budget splits** across variants
- Don't kill ads too early — let the data breathe (3–7 days minimum)

---

## Metrics to Watch

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| CTR | Is the hook/headline working? |
| CPC | Is the audience match + copy quality good? |
| CVR | Is the body copy + CTA convincing? |
| ROAS | Is the full funnel working? |
| Thumb-Stop Rate | (Video) Does the first second work? |
| Hook Rate | (Video) Are people watching past 3 seconds? |

---

## How the Platform Supports Testing

- The [[AI Suggestions Engine]] generates multiple variants automatically
- Winning copy patterns are stored and inform future suggestions
- Platform benchmarks ([[Industry Benchmarks]]) flag underperformers
- Copy critique tool explains why a variant may be underperforming

---

## Iteration Principles

- **Document everything** — know why winners won, not just that they did
- **Transfer learnings** — a hook that works on Meta may work on TikTok
- **Seasonal resets** — creative fatigue sets in after 4–6 weeks; refresh regularly
