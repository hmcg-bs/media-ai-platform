## Core Idea

Copywriting is the craft of writing text that persuades a reader to take a specific action. In the context of ads, every word must earn its place — the goal is clarity, relevance, and emotional resonance.

---

## Key Principles

1. **Speak to one person** — Write as if talking directly to a single reader, not a crowd.
2. **Lead with the benefit, not the feature** — "Save 2 hours a day" beats "Automated scheduling."
3. **Match the voice to the platform** — TikTok copy is not LinkedIn copy.
4. **Short sentences move faster** — Rhythm matters. Breaks create emphasis.
5. **Specificity builds trust** — "47% more clicks" is more believable than "way more clicks."

---

## The Copy Stack

| Layer | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Stop the scroll | "Most founders get this wrong..." |
| Body | Build desire / address pain | Problem → Agitate → Solution |
| CTA | Direct the action | "Start free — no card needed" |

---

## Common Mistakes

- Writing for yourself instead of the reader
- Burying the lead (the benefit comes too late)
- Weak or generic CTAs ("Click here", "Learn more")
- Ignoring the emotional driver behind the purchase
