## Core Idea

Performance ads are paid placements optimised for a measurable outcome — clicks, conversions, purchases, sign-ups. Every element (headline, visual, CTA) is a variable that can be tested and improved.

---

## Platforms

| Platform | Primary Format | Key Copy Element |
|---|---|---|
| Meta (Facebook/Instagram) | Feed, Stories, Reels | Hook + short body + CTA |
| Google Search | Text ads | Headline match to search intent |
| Google Display | Banner/responsive | Punchy headline, visual anchor |
| TikTok | In-feed video | First 3 seconds (hook critical) |
| LinkedIn | Sponsored content | Credibility-led, professional tone |
| YouTube | Pre-roll / mid-roll | Skip-proof first 5 seconds |

---

## Copy Structure for Performance Ads

1. **Hook** — Pattern interrupt. Makes the viewer stop. ([[Hook Writing]])
2. **Value Proposition** — Why this, why now, why you?
3. **Social Proof** — Reviews, numbers, logos
4. **CTA** — Single, clear action ([[CTA Writing]])

---

## What the AI Should Help With

- Generating multiple headline variants for A/B testing
- Adapting copy tone for different platforms
- Suggesting hooks based on audience pain points ([[Audience Targeting]])
- Flagging weak CTAs and proposing alternatives
- Benchmarking copy against [[Industry Benchmarks]]

---

## Key Metrics to Track

- CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- CPC (Cost Per Click)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- CVR (Conversion Rate)
- Quality Score (Google) / Relevance Score (Meta)
