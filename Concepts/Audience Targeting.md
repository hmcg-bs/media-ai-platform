# Audience Targeting

**Type:** Concept Note

---

## Core Idea

Copy without audience clarity is guesswork. The more precisely you understand who you're writing for — their pain, language, awareness level, and platform behaviour — the more relevant and effective the copy becomes.

---

## Audience Dimensions

| Dimension | Questions to Answer |
|---|---|
| Demographics | Age, gender, location, income, job title |
| Psychographics | Values, aspirations, fears, identity |
| Problem Awareness | Do they know they have the problem? Do they know solutions exist? |
| Platform Behaviour | How do they consume content here? Scroll fast or slow? |
| Language | What words do they use to describe their pain? |

---

## Awareness Levels (Eugene Schwartz)

Understanding where an audience sits on the awareness spectrum changes what copy should say:

| Level | State | Copy Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Doesn't know they have a problem | Educate, story-led |
| Problem-Aware | Knows the problem, not solutions | [[PAS Framework]] — agitate the pain |
| Solution-Aware | Knows solutions exist, not yours | Differentiate, compare |
| Product-Aware | Knows your product, hasn't bought | Overcome objections, add proof |
| Most Aware | Ready to buy | Offer, urgency, CTA |

## Audience Research Sources

- Customer interviews and sales call notes
- Reddit / Quora threads in your niche
- 1-star competitor reviews (reveals unmet needs)
- Facebook/Instagram comment sections on competitor ads
- Your own DMs and support tickets
